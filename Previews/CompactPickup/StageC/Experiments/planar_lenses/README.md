# Rejected planar-lens experiment

This is **not the final vehicle**. The candidate used single exposed front sheets, retaining standard Light role/crumple 0.45. It had 84,068 Blender and 110,976 Unity deformable vertices. All unchanged mandatory Blender checks passed; the front indicator disappeared from the numerical fold-outlier list.

Actual Unity close-ups still showed the indicator wing. Oblique undamaged renders also revealed reduced lens depth, with the flat planes standing ahead of their housings. A front-only <5 mm surface comparison excluded that side-depth difference and therefore did not prove unchanged appearance. The experiment was rejected; final lenses restore the fuller approved shape.
